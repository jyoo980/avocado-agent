  Key toolchain findings (in cbmc/README.md)

  DFCC needed three non-obvious things: baking the entry point at goto-cc 
  --function harness time, a --generate-function-body 'free|malloc' pre-pass
  (otherwise the contracts library's inlined free triggers a fatal "no body for
  free"), and keeping --apply-loop-contracts opt-in (it otherwise rejects
  loop-index writes in un-annotated loops).

  Honest scope notes

  - This covers ~32 of ~95 functions. The remainder are materially harder:
  unchecked malloc paths (fail under CBMC's may-fail model), floating-point +
  log() (ZopfliCalculateEntropy), recursion and function pointers, and deep
  realloc-based dynamic arrays — each needs substantially more
  loop-invariant/heap engineering.

  inner assertion needs a quantified array-match precondition, but CBMC emits
  the array-bounds check for the quantifier body regardless of the ==> guard, so
  it wouldn't discharge. I reverted it rather than log a failing command.
